6. Detailed Interpretation

StepAssignmentExpected from composition (wt%)CommentRefs
IFree water of the paste + physisorbed water in the silica gel pores + interlayer water of bentoniteWater added: 68.99Largest step: one broad DTG peak at 138 °C that ends sharply at ~175 °C, when the last liquid water leaves the 150 Å silica pores. Zhuravlev puts the end of physisorbed-water removal from silica at 190 ± 10 °C; smectite interlayer water also leaves below ~200 °C. After this step the dry paste is 31.79 wt% of the loaded mass.[1], [2], [4]
IIOxidative burn-off of methyl cellulose + condensation of vicinal (hydrogen-bonded) silanols1.29 = methyl cellulose 0.93 + vicinal-silanol water 0.36Small DTG peak at 288 °C with a long tail to ~500 °C. In N2, methyl cellulose has T10 ≈ 292 °C and decomposes at ~343 °C; in air it oxidises at similar or lower temperature, which matches the peak. Vicinal silanols condense (2 Si–OH → Si–O–Si + H2O) mainly between 200 and 450 °C and are removed by ~400 °C; this water gives the long tail. Expressed on the dry paste, step II = 4.53 %.[1], [2], [5], [6]
IIIDehydroxylation of bentonite + slow condensation of isolated silanols0.23 = bentonite OH 0.10 + isolated-silanol water 0.13Very small loss with a weak DTG peak at 582 °C, typical of the dehydroxylation of montmorillonite (4.7–4.9 % of the clay mass). Isolated silanols condense slowly up to and beyond 900 °C, so the mass is still falling slightly at 900 °C. Expressed on the dry paste, step III = 0.88 %.[1], [4]
